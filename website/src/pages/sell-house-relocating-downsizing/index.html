One less thing to manage during a big move

Moving is a lot on its own. Getting a house ready to sell at the same time means repairs, staging, keeping it spotless for showings, and not knowing when or if it will sell. If you've already moved, you may be paying for two homes while you wait.

We give you a firm cash offer and a closing date you choose, so you can plan the move with certainty.

How we make relocating easier

  • Close when it suits you. As little as 7 business days, or weeks later once you've settled into the new place.
  • No showings. One walkthrough, and then you can pack without keeping the house show-ready.
  • Downsizing? Take what fits in your new home and leave the rest. We handle the cleanout.
  • Already moved away? We can coordinate access and paperwork remotely.

Our honest take: if you have time before you move and the house is in great shape, the open market may bring more money. If you want certainty and less to manage, our offer is worth comparing.
