The hidden cost of an empty house

A house nobody lives in still costs money every month: property taxes, insurance, utilities, yard care and repairs. It can also attract break-ins, squatters, vandalism and code complaints.

Insurance can be a problem too. Many standard homeowner policies limit coverage once a home has been vacant for a while, so check with your insurance company about your policy.

Vacant homes we buy

  • Homes left empty after a move, a passing or a long-term care placement
  • Former rentals between tenants that need work
  • Homes with break-in, vandalism or weather damage
  • Properties with code violations or city notices
  • Houses that have sat on the market without selling

Selling from out of the area

If you live far away, we can coordinate access with a lockbox, a neighbor or a family member, and handle most of the closing paperwork remotely through the title company. You don't need to fly in to sell.

No cleanout needed: if the house still has furniture or belongings, leave them. We'll handle it after closing.
