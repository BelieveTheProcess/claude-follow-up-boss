Selling a loved one's home is hard. The sale doesn't have to be.

When a parent or relative passes, the house often comes with decades of belongings, repairs nobody has made in years, and family members in different places who all need to agree. On top of that, the taxes, insurance and utilities keep coming while the house sits.

We're a local, family-run company, and we treat these sales with care. We buy the home exactly as it is, so your family doesn't have to clean it out, fix it up or hold open houses.

How we help heirs and executors

  • Leave the belongings. Take the photos, keepsakes and anything else that matters. We handle the rest after closing.
  • Work with your attorney. If the estate is in probate, we work within the court process and with the estate's attorney or personal representative.
  • Keep it simple for everyone. One clear offer with the comps behind it makes it easier for siblings and co-heirs to agree.
  • Out-of-town heirs. We can coordinate access, the walkthrough and closing paperwork so you don't have to fly in for every step.
  • Pick your timing. Close quickly, or wait until the estate is ready. The date is up to you.

Trust, probate, or neither?

How the house is held changes the process. Homes held in a living trust can often be sold by the successor trustee without going to court. Homes that pass through probate may need court involvement before or during the sale. Every estate is different, so talk with an estate attorney about your situation. We'll work within whatever process applies.

A note on taxes: California's property tax rules for inherited homes changed in recent years, and selling an inherited home can have income tax effects too. We're not tax advisors, so please check with a tax professional before you decide.

What happens next

  1. Call or fill out the form. Tell us about the property and who's involved in the decision.

  3. When the estate is ready, we close with a trusted local title company and the proceeds go where the estate directs.
