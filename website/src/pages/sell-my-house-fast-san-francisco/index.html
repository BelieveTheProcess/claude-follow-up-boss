Why San Francisco owners sell to us

In San Francisco, even a straightforward sale can take a lot of preparation, and occupied or older homes are harder still. Buyers' inspections often turn up foundation, seismic or permit questions that stall a deal.

We buy as-is with no financing contingency. Tenants, in-law units and old systems are factored into our offer up front, so there are no surprises late in the process.

Our honest take: a renovated, vacant San Francisco home will usually do well on the open market. If yours is occupied, older, or you want a simple, certain sale, get our number to compare.
