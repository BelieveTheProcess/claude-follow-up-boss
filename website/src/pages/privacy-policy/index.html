Privacy policy

Effective September 24, 2026

{{> roofline}}

This policy explains how Believe The Process Ventures LLC ("we," "us") collects, uses and protects information when you visit believetheprocess.com or contact us. We buy houses directly from homeowners. We do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: when you fill out our form, call, text or email us, we collect details such as your name, phone number, email address, property address, your timeline and situation, and anything else you choose to share.
  • Information collected automatically: like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit and the page that referred you. If you arrive from an ad, our form may record campaign details from the link (for example, UTM parameters).

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request, schedule a walkthrough and make you an offer on your property
  • To contact you by phone, text message or email about your property
  • To complete a purchase if you decide to sell to us, including sharing necessary details with the title and escrow company
  • To keep records of our communications and your consent
  • To improve our website and understand which of our marketing works

Text messaging

If you provide your phone number and agree to be contacted, we may call or text you about your property, including with automated technology. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. You can also email us to opt out. Consent to receive calls or texts is not a condition of receiving an offer or selling your property.

We do not share mobile phone numbers or text messaging opt-in data or consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

How we share information

We share information only as needed to run our business and serve you:

  • Service providers who help us operate, such as our website host, form and automation tools, customer relationship management (CRM) software, and phone, text and email providers. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Title and escrow companies, lenders being paid off, and similar parties involved in a sale you agree to.
  • Legal requirements, when required by law, subpoena or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for other companies' marketing.

Your choices and California privacy rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we have about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can ask us to stop contacting you. Email us at believetheprocess@btpventuresllc.com or call (415) 770-0722. We will not treat you differently for making a request. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it, such as records of a completed sale.

Cookies and third-party services

Our site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we'll update this policy to describe them.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't send sensitive information such as bank or Social Security numbers through our website form. We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law.

Children

Our website is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
