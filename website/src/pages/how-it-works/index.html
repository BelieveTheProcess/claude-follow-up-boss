Three steps from call to closing

  1. Tell us about your home

    Fill out the short form or call (415) 770-0722. We'll ask about the property, its condition and your timeline, and set up a time to see it.

  2. Review your offer

What happens at the walkthrough

We'll meet you at the house at a time that works for you. It usually takes less than an hour. We look at the condition of the roof, foundation, plumbing, electrical and anything else that might need work, and we'll ask you about anything you know about the home.

You don't need to clean, stage or fix anything beforehand. We want to see the house as it really is so our offer is accurate.

How we come up with our offer

We believe you deserve to see the numbers, so there's no mystery. Our offer comes from:

  • Recent nearby sales of similar homes, the comps, which we share with you.
  • The home's size, age and layout, including bedrooms and bathrooms.
  • The cost of repairs we'd need to make after we buy it.
  • Our costs to buy, hold and resell the home, and a fair margin for the risk we take on.

Honest numbers: if a traditional sale would clearly net you more, we'll tell you. Our goal is to help you make the right decision, even if that means not selling to us.

What closing looks like

Once you accept, we open escrow with a trusted local title company. They handle the paperwork, check the title, pay off any mortgage or liens, and send your proceeds to you at closing. We'll go over every closing cost in writing before you sign anything.

You pick the date. We can close in as little as 7 business days once title is clear, or later if you need time to move. Take what you want from the house and leave the rest.

What you don't have to do

  • Make repairs or renovations
  • Clean, stage or empty the house
  • Hold open houses or schedule showings
  • Wait on a buyer's loan approval
  • Pay commissions on a direct sale
