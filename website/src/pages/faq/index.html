Getting an offer

How fast can I get an offer? +

We reply the same business day and usually have a written cash offer ready within 24 hours of seeing the property.

Does it cost anything to get an offer? +

No. The offer is free and there's no obligation to accept it.

Do you need to see the house? +

Yes. We do one walkthrough so our offer is accurate. There are no showings or open houses beyond that.

Am I obligated to accept? +

Never. You can say no, take time to think, or compare it with other options.

Pricing and costs

How do you decide what to offer? +

We look at recent nearby sales, the size and age of the home, bedrooms and bathrooms, and the cost of any repairs, plus our costs to buy, hold and resell. We show you the comps we used so the number makes sense.

Are there fees or commissions? +

There's no commission on a direct cash sale to us. We'll go over every closing cost in writing before you sign anything.

Why would I sell for less than full market value? +

A cash sale trades some price for speed, certainty and convenience: no repairs, no showings, no commissions and no buyer financing that can fall through. If a traditional sale would clearly net you more and you have the time, we'll tell you.

Will you lower the price after the walkthrough? +

Our offer is based on what we see during the walkthrough. We'd only revisit it if something major and unknown came up, and we'd walk you through it honestly.

The sale and closing

How fast can you close? +

In as little as 7 business days once we agree on a price and title is clear, or on whatever later date you choose.

Who handles the paperwork? +

We work with a trusted local title company. They handle escrow, pay off any mortgage or liens, and send your proceeds to you at closing.

Do I need to clean or fix anything? +

No. Take what you want and leave the rest. We buy the home as-is.

Can I sell if I still owe money on the house? +

Yes. Your mortgage is paid off from the sale proceeds at closing, and the rest goes to you.

Can I sell if I live out of the area? +

Yes. We can coordinate access and much of the paperwork remotely through the title company.

Special situations

Do you buy houses with tenants in them? +

Yes. We buy rentals with tenants in place and plan the sale around the leases and local tenant rules.

Can you buy an inherited or probate house? +

Often, yes. We work with executors, trustees and estate attorneys, and within the court process if the estate is in probate.

What if I'm behind on payments? +

Call early. A quick sale may let you pay off the loan before a foreclosure sale. We'll also tell you about other options, like talking to your lender or a free HUD-approved housing counselor.

Where do you buy? +

Santa Clara, Alameda, San Mateo, San Francisco and Contra Costa counties. Don't see your city? Call us anyway.
