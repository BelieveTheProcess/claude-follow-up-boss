You don't need to fix it to sell it

In the Bay Area, repairs are expensive and contractors book up fast. Getting an older house ready for the open market can take months and a lot of money up front, and a buyer's inspection can still turn up new problems and new negotiations.

We buy houses in any condition. We walk the home once, estimate the repairs ourselves, and build that into a single cash offer. After closing, the repairs are our job, not yours.

Problems we see all the time

  • Foundation cracks, settling or seismic retrofit needs
  • Roof leaks, dry rot and water damage
  • Old electrical panels, knob-and-tube wiring or galvanized plumbing
  • Sewer lateral failures and drainage problems
  • Fire or smoke damage
  • Mold and moisture issues
  • Unpermitted additions, garage conversions or in-law units
  • Hoarding situations or homes that need a full cleanout

How we price repairs: we start from what similar homes nearby have sold for once fixed up, then subtract our repair estimate and our costs. We walk you through those numbers so the offer makes sense.

Why skip the repairs?

  • No money up front. You don't pay contractors before you sell.
  • No surprises. There's no buyer inspection repair list or last-minute credit request.
  • No delays. Close in as little as 7 business days instead of waiting on permits and crews.
