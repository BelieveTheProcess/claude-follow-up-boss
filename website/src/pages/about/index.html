Why "Believe The Process"

Our name is how we work. Selling a house is often tied to something bigger: a loss in the family, a move, a divorce, money trouble. We can't change what brought you here, but we can make the sale itself simple, honest and calm.

Believing the process means doing the right thing at every step: telling people the truth about their options, showing our numbers, keeping our word on dates, and trusting that treating people well is the best way to build a business.

What we stand for

  • Faith and family. We're a family business, and we treat every seller the way we'd want our own parents treated.
  • Honest numbers. We show you the comps and the repair costs behind our offer, so the number makes sense.
  • No pressure, ever. Take your time, talk it over, compare your options. The offer isn't a trap, and you can say no.
  • Transparency. We're the buyer, and like any buyer we aim to make a fair profit. We'd rather tell you that up front.
  • Keeping our word. When we commit to a price and a date, we work hard to deliver on both.

Who we help

We buy houses in Santa Clara, Alameda, San Mateo, San Francisco and Contra Costa counties, with Santa Clara County as our main market. We help families with inherited homes, owners with houses that need major repairs, landlords who are ready to be done, people facing foreclosure, and anyone who values a simple, certain sale over the traditional process.

Our honest promise: if selling to us isn't the right move for you, we'll say so, even if it means we don't buy your house.
