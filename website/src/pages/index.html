Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do you come up with your offer?

We look at what similar homes nearby have sold for once fixed up, subtract the repairs the house needs and our costs to buy, hold and resell it, and leave room for a fair profit. We walk you through each piece so you can see exactly how we got to the number.

Are there any fees or commissions?

No commissions. The offer we make is the number we’re offering for your house, and we’ll go over what you should expect to walk away with before you sign anything.

How fast can you close?

In as little as 7 business days once we agree on a price and title is clear. If you need more time, we’ll close on whatever date you choose.

Do I need to fix or clean anything?

No. We buy houses as-is, in any condition. Take the things you want and leave the rest. We’ll handle the cleanout.

Am I obligated to accept your offer?

Not at all. Getting an offer is free and there’s no pressure. If it doesn’t work for you, no hard feelings.

Will there be showings or open houses?

No. We just need one visit to see the property so we can make an accurate offer. That’s it.
