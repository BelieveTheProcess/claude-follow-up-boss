Common questions

How fast can I get an offer? +

We reply the same business day and usually have a cash offer ready within 24 hours of seeing the property.

How do you decide what to offer? +

We look at recent nearby sales, the size and age of the home, bedrooms and bathrooms, and the cost of any repairs. We show you the comps we used so the number makes sense.

Do I have to clean or fix anything? +

No. Take what you want and leave the rest. We buy the home as-is.

Are there fees? +

There's no commission on a direct cash sale to us. We'll go over every closing cost in writing before you sign anything.

Am I obligated to accept? +

Never. The offer is free and you can say no, take time to think, or compare it with other options.
