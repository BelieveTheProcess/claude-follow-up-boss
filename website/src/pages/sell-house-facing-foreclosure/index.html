Falling behind happens. What matters is acting early.

Job changes, medical bills, a divorce, a loan that reset higher. There are plenty of reasons homeowners fall behind, and none of them make you a bad person. What matters now is time, because every month that passes can mean fewer options and more fees.

We're a local, family-run cash buyer. If selling is the right move, we can close fast enough to pay off the loan before a foreclosure sale, often leaving you with money in your pocket instead of losing the equity you've built.

How foreclosure generally works in California

Most California foreclosures are handled outside of court. In general, the lender records a Notice of Default, waits at least three months, then records a Notice of Trustee's Sale and sets a sale date at least a few weeks out. Timelines vary by lender and situation, but the sooner you act, the more room there is to choose.

Your options, honestly

  • Talk to your lender. Ask about a repayment plan, forbearance or a loan modification. Some homeowners catch up this way.
  • Get free help. HUD-approved housing counselors offer free advice. You can find one through HUD's website or by calling the number on your mortgage statement.
  • Sell before the sale date. If you have equity and keeping the house isn't realistic, a quick cash sale pays off the loan and can protect your credit from a completed foreclosure.

Our promise: we'll tell you honestly if we think another option is better for you. We'd rather lose a deal than push someone into the wrong decision.

Common questions

Can I sell my house if I've already received a Notice of Default? +

Usually, yes. You generally have the right to sell until the foreclosure sale happens. The earlier you act, the easier it is to close in time.

What if I owe more than the house is worth? +

A cash sale may not cover the full loan. In that case, talk to your lender and a HUD-approved housing counselor about options like a short sale. We'll tell you honestly if our offer won't cover your payoff.

Will selling stop the foreclosure? +

If the sale closes and the loan is paid off before the foreclosure sale date, the foreclosure ends because the debt is paid.

Is this confidential? +

Yes. There are no signs, no public open houses and no photos posted online.
