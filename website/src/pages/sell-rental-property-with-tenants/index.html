Sell your rental without the move-out headache

Selling a rental the traditional way usually means waiting for a lease to end, working through tenant protection rules, repairing and painting after move-out, and carrying an empty property while it's on the market. In the Bay Area, that can take months.

We buy rentals with the tenants still living there. We take over the leases at closing and handle the transition ourselves, so you don't have to.

Rental situations we help with

  • Tenants who pay late or not at all
  • Long-term tenants paying well below market rent
  • Properties that need major repairs you don't want to fund
  • Small multi-units and homes with in-law units
  • Owners who live far away and are tired of managing from a distance
  • Properties inherited with tenants already in place

Tenant rules matter: California's statewide rent cap and just-cause rules apply to many rentals, and cities like San Jose, Oakland, San Francisco, Hayward and Mountain View have their own ordinances on top. We plan every purchase around the lease and the tenants' rights. We treat tenants with respect.

How it works

  1. Tell us about the property, the leases and the rents.
  2. We schedule a walkthrough with proper notice to the tenants and give you a written cash offer within 24 hours.
  3. At closing, deposits and leases transfer to us through the title company, and we become the landlord.
