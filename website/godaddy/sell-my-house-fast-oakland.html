We buy houses in Oakland

Oakland has some of the oldest and most varied housing in the Bay Area, and that's exactly the kind of home we buy. We're a local, family-run business, and we see every property in person before we make an offer.

No staging, no open houses and no waiting on a buyer's lender. You get a clear offer and choose when to close.

Oakland homes we buy

Many Oakland homes are 80 to 120 years old. The properties we see most often:

  • Victorians and craftsman homes with original foundations, knob-and-tube wiring or old plumbing.
  • Rentals and small multi-units with tenants. Oakland has rent adjustment and just-cause eviction rules that make selling a rental complicated. We can buy with tenants in place.
  • Hillside homes with foundation, drainage or access challenges.
  • Homes with code violations or open permits that need to be resolved.
  • Inherited family homes with decades of belongings.

Why Oakland sellers choose a cash buyer

Older homes and rental properties are where traditional sales most often hit trouble. Inspection reports get long, lenders ask for repairs, and tenant situations make showings difficult or impossible.

We take the property as it is: tenants, violations, old systems and all. You get one number, and the repairs and paperwork become our job after closing.

Our honest take: if your Oakland home is renovated and vacant, the open market may get you more. If it's older, occupied or needs work, our offer gives you a certain, simple option to compare.

How selling your Oakland house works

  1. Tell us about your home. Fill out the form or call (415) 770-0722. We'll ask a few questions about the property and your timeline.
